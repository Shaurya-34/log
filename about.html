About

Updated August 2026

Hi, I'm Shaurya. I write software, and this log is where I keep notes on whatever I'm building or curious about, mostly figuring it out as I go. The subjects move around: machine learning, graphics, programming, computation, mathematical ideas, and small experiments that are easier to understand by implementing them than by reading about them.

The point of the site is not to present polished documentation or a fixed set of opinions. Most posts are working notes: I start with a question, follow the interesting parts, build something when code helps, and write down what survived the experiment. Some pieces are explanatory, some exploratory, and some are simply records of a rabbit hole that seemed worth keeping.

There is no analytics layer, comments system, newsletter, or account system on the site. The pages are static files, and the machine-readable Markdown representations are published alongside the HTML pages. The Contact page explains how to reach me, while the Privacy page describes the site's data practices.
